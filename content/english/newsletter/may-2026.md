Spring arrived properly in Gothenburg this month. The kind where you actually believe it's staying. We've been spending most of our free time in the garden — which right now means moving a lot of earth and gravel rather than enjoying it. Twelve tons of macadam sitting on the driveway will do that to a weekend.

## Highlights from last month

A herb garden and an extra raised bed are finally in — the second one didn't quite make it last year. Groundwork has started for a greenhouse and a garden shed, and a gravel path is taking shape. It's the kind of project where progress is measured in wheelbarrow loads.

Took the family to Liseberg for the day. We spent most of it in Kaninlandet with the kids. Kaffekoppen and Raballder were the clear favourites — the kids asked to go on both about four times each.

On the writing side, I published two new pieces: [The Page Unprinted](/writing/the-page-unprinted/), about the strange and often overlooked world of CSS print styling, and [The Grid, Inherited](/writing/the-grid-inherited/), on layout systems and what we carry forward from print into digital.

I've also been working on a custom HTML-based presentation tool — a way around the limitations I keep running into with existing options. Custom typefaces, OS dependencies, licensing — it adds up. More on that when it's further along.
